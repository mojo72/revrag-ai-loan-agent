# Demo video script (5 to 7 minutes)

Focus only on the agent and what it does. No architecture or feedback talk. Record in Chrome with system audio on so Sara's voice is captured. Every refresh starts a fresh, empty application.

Sara runs on the RevRag agent through RevRag's floating agent button. She guides by voice and you fill the form as she instructs.

| Time | You do / say | What the viewer should see |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open the live link. Tap RevRag's agent button in the bottom-right corner and allow the microphone. | The RevRag voice call connects and Sara greets you. |
| 0:20 | "What kinds of loans do you have?" | Sara lists the four products briefly. |
| 0:40 | "What's the interest rate on a personal loan, and how long can I take it for?" | Rate range and 12 to 72 months, from the knowledge base. |
| 1:00 | **"I want a 5 lakh personal loan for 3 years."** | Sara confirms and tells you what to fill on Loan details. Fill product, amount, tenure as she says. |
| 1:20 | "It's for a family wedding." Then press Continue. | Sara asks for the eligibility details. |
| 1:40 | "What will my EMI be?" | An approximate EMI, with the caveat that the final rate depends on the credit check. |
| 2:00 | Give date of birth, salaried, 85,000 take-home, no other loans, CIBIL around 760; fill them as she guides and press Check eligibility. | Eligibility result card: eligible up to 13 lakh; Sara explains it. |
| 2:30 | "Can I take 15 lakh instead?" | Sara explains the 13 lakh limit and offers a lower amount or a longer tenure. |
| 2:50 | On Personal info, ask: **"What do I need to enter here?"** | Context-aware guidance for this exact screen. |
| 3:10 | Fill name, PAN, email and mobile. On the Bank screen type the IFSC as HDFC1234567 and press Continue. | **Error recovery:** the IFSC error appears; Sara explains the 5th character must be zero. Fix it. |
| 4:20 | On Documents, press Continue with nothing uploaded. | Sara explains which documents are needed; use "Use sample documents (demo)". |
| 4:50 | On Review: "Can you summarise my application?" | A short summary of product, amount, tenure and EMI. |
| 5:20 | Sara asks for consents; say yes, tick both boxes, and submit. | Confirmation screen with the application ID. |
| 5:50 | "What happens next, and how long will approval take?" | The post-submission steps and timelines. |

Tips: speak naturally and pause briefly after each request; you can talk over Sara to interrupt her. Open the Status pill before recording to confirm the RevRag SDK is initialised and the customer is identified.
